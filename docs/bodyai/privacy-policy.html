BodyAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

概述

欢迎使用 BodyAI。本政策说明我们如何处理您使用体态、肌肉、动作评估与个人模特功能时的信息，以及您可以使用的数据管理方式。BodyAI 无需注册账户即可使用本地功能。照片、身体资料与分析结果可能涉及敏感个人信息，请仅提供您有权使用的素材。

我们处理的信息

身体资料：您填写的性别、年龄、身高和体重，用于本地档案与相关展示。 评估素材与结果：您拍摄或选择的照片、视频、本地姿态关键点、测量数值、报告及云端返回的分析结果。 个人模特：您主动选择的人脸照片、全身照片、档案性别及生成的形象图片。 购买与权益：Apple 提供的交易及订阅状态、恢复购买结果和本地分析次数记录；本应用不接收银行卡信息。 服务与诊断：云端配置服务使用随机安装标识及访客令牌；联网服务还会接收 IP 地址等必要网络信息。应用可能记录请求标识、错误、耗时、图片尺寸，以及故障时的分析返回内容，用于问题排查。

本地处理与云端上传

照片、视频、身体资料和报告主要保存在本机。本地姿态识别、测量与浏览记录不需要上传完整素材。 当前版本的「云端解读」默认开启。开启时，新体态和肌肉评估会自动上传本次相关照片、测量值及必要的评估元数据，供云端服务生成分析。视频评估需手动发起，仅上传精选视频帧和相关测量数据，不上传原始视频。查看历史报告不会自动重新分析。 您可以在「我的」关闭「允许云端解读」，停止后续评估上传。关闭开关不能撤回已发送的数据，也不等同于取消个人模特生成。

个人模特生成

个人模特生成需要您另行选择照片并主动发起。人脸照片、全身照片、性别及生成所需的模板或提示词会发送至已配置的图像生成服务；根据服务配置，可能经中转服务转发给上游模型提供方。照片可能包含可识别的面部信息。 生成图片用于首页形象展示，不作为体态测量或医疗判断依据。您可在个人模特页面取消任务、放弃预览或「删除并恢复默认」。已发往中转或上游的数据不能通过删除本机图片直接撤回。

权限与第三方服务

相机：用于您主动拍摄照片或动作视频；系统照片选择器或相册权限：用于导入您选择的素材。可在 iOS 设置中管理权限，拒绝后相关功能可能无法使用。 Apple StoreKit 负责购买与订阅；Apple Vision 与 MediaPipe 用于设备端视觉处理。云端配置服务 api.aishotball.com 提供访客鉴权及模型配置；当前内置分析中转地址为 api.a6api.vip，图像生成配置可使用 api.a6api.com。实际接收素材的服务取决于当次生效配置，中转服务可能进一步调用上游 AI 提供方。 第三方处理的数据、保存地点、保留期限和模型训练规则取决于其实际服务条款。我们不承诺所有上游均为零留存；如您不希望照片发往第三方，请关闭云端解读并避免发起个人模特生成。

用途、共享与安全

上述信息用于提供您使用的评估与生成服务、保存记录、管理购买权益以及排查故障。我们不出售您的个人信息，不使用这些信息进行广告定向投放或跨应用追踪。 实现云端分析和图像生成时，必要素材会提供给相关技术服务商；在法律要求或保障合法权益确有必要时，也可能披露必要信息。网络请求使用 HTTPS。设备访问限制、钥匙串及应用私有存储等措施有助于保护数据，但任何系统均无法保证绝对安全。

保存期限与删除

本地报告和素材保留至您删除或卸载应用。您可在「我的」使用「清除全部报告和素材」删除评估记录；此操作不清除身体档案、个人模特或购买权益记录。身体资料可在「身体资料」中修改，个人模特需在其独立页面管理。钥匙串中的权益记录可能在卸载后保留；删除应用不会取消 Apple 订阅。导出到系统相册或分享至其他应用的副本须另行删除。 云端及上游服务可能保留处理记录和必要日志；本地删除不会同步删除上游副本。具体保留期限、处理地区及删除能力须按实际服务配置核实。如需查询或申请删除已上传的数据，请通过下述邮箱联系，并提供大致时间、功能及已有请求标识，避免重复发送敏感照片。

您的选择

您可以修改身体档案、删除评估记录、删除个人模特、关闭云端解读，以及在 iOS 设置中撤销权限。订阅可在 Apple 的订阅管理页面管理。 如需查询、更正、删除相关信息或提出隐私问题，请联系我们。我们会根据可核验的请求与实际服务能力处理，并说明无法直接处理的第三方数据或依法必须保留的信息。

未成年人和政策更新

未成年人请在监护人指导与同意下使用涉及身体照片、云端分析或形象生成的功能。请勿上传未经授权的他人照片。 功能或数据处理方式发生变化时，我们会更新本政策并标注日期，涉及重大变化时提供适当提示。BodyAI 用于身体观察与记录，不能替代专业医疗评估。

Overview

Welcome to BodyAI. This policy explains how information is handled for posture, muscle and movement assessments and personal model generation, and how you can manage it. Local features do not require an account. Body photos, profiles and assessment results may contain sensitive personal information. Only provide media you are authorized to use.

Information we process

Body profile: sex, age, height and weight you enter for your local profile and related displays. Assessment media and results: photos and videos you capture or select, local pose landmarks, measurements, reports and cloud analysis results. Personal model: face and full-body photos you select, profile sex and generated portraits. Purchases: Apple transaction and subscription status, restore results and local analysis usage records. The App does not receive bank card details. Service and diagnostics: a random installation identifier and guest token for cloud configuration; online services also receive necessary network information such as your IP address. Diagnostic logs may include request identifiers, errors, timings, image sizes and analysis response content on failures.

Local processing and cloud uploads

Photos, videos, profiles and reports are primarily stored on your device. Local pose detection, measurements and browsing saved reports do not require uploading full media. Cloud interpretation is enabled by default in the current version. When enabled, new posture and muscle assessments automatically upload relevant photos, measurements and necessary assessment metadata for cloud analysis. Video assessment requires a manual cloud request and uploads selected frames and measurements, not the original video. Viewing saved reports does not automatically request another analysis. Turn off Allow cloud interpretation under My Space to stop future assessment uploads. This cannot recall data already sent and does not cancel personal model generation.

Personal model generation

You separately select photos and actively start generation. Face and full-body photos, profile sex and required templates or prompts are sent to the configured image generation service. Depending on configuration, a relay may forward them to an upstream AI provider. Photos may contain identifiable facial information. The result is a homepage illustration, not measurement or medical evidence. You can cancel a task, discard a preview or delete the model and restore the default. Deleting local images cannot recall data already sent to a relay or upstream provider.

Permissions and third-party services

Camera access supports photos and movement recording. The system photo picker or Photos permission supports importing selected media. Manage permissions in iOS Settings; denying access may limit the relevant feature. Apple StoreKit handles purchases and subscriptions. Apple Vision and MediaPipe provide on-device vision processing. api.aishotball.com provides guest authentication and cloud configuration. The current built-in analysis relay is api.a6api.vip; image generation can be configured to use api.a6api.com. Actual recipients depend on the configuration effective for the request. Relays may call upstream AI providers. Third-party retention, processing locations and model training rules depend on their actual terms. We do not promise zero retention across all upstream services. If you do not want photos sent to third parties, disable cloud interpretation and do not start personal model generation.

Use, sharing and security

Information is used to deliver requested assessments and generation, save records, manage purchase entitlements and troubleshoot failures. We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising or cross-app tracking. Cloud analysis and image generation require sharing necessary inputs with technical service providers. Necessary disclosures may also occur when legally required or needed to protect legitimate rights. Network requests use HTTPS. Device access controls, Keychain and private app storage help protect data; no system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Local assessment reports and media remain until you delete them or uninstall the App. Clear all reports and media under My Space deletes assessment records, not body profiles, personal models or purchase entitlements. Edit profile details separately and manage personal models on their own screen. Keychain entitlement records may survive uninstalling. Uninstalling does not cancel Apple subscriptions. Copies exported to Photos or other apps must be deleted separately. Cloud and upstream services may retain processing records and necessary logs. Local deletion does not remove upstream copies. Exact retention periods, processing regions and deletion capabilities require verification against the actual service configuration. To request access or deletion of uploaded data, email us with the approximate time, feature and any existing request identifier. Avoid resending sensitive photos.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, delete assessment records and personal models, disable cloud interpretation and revoke permissions in iOS Settings. Manage subscriptions through Apple subscription settings. Contact us for access, correction, deletion or privacy questions. We will handle verifiable requests within the actual capabilities of the service and explain limitations involving third-party data or legally required retention.

Minors and updates

Minors should use features involving body photos, cloud analysis or image generation with guardian guidance and consent. Do not upload unauthorized photos of other people. We will update this policy and its date when features or data practices change, with appropriate notice for material changes. BodyAI supports personal observation and records and does not replace professional medical assessment.
